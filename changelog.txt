v0.2.0.0 - The Rare Jewels Update
(Beta)

--- ADDITIONS ---
- New "Rare" jewels that have special behavior, or entirely new functionality.

Rare Jewels (and where they drop most often):
AMBER (Desert): Either Full or Pure, allows a jewel to slot in any accessory instead of providing random stats - Full is pre-filled, Pure is fillable

ANCIENT (Marble/Granite): Major/Minor, less total cuts and much less likely cuts BUT much, much stronger upgrades per cut

CAT'S EYE (Underworld): Major/Minor, instead of granting stats they boost a material set's count. This allows set bonuses to be achieved easier without using more jewelry

CURSED (Corruption/Crimson): Major/Minor, alongside normal stats, all but one of their stats may be negative, which reduce their associated stat but multiply the positive stats on the jewel accordingly
Multiplier is this:
	((total negative stats * -2) / total positive stats) + 1
The more negative stats (or less positive stats), the stronger the boost.

GELID (Hallow): Major/Minor, normal jewels but they're way easier to upgrade...and way weaker as a result

IMPURE: A special case, Impure jewels are Major/Minor and hold stats like normal jewels, but disregard class restrictions. 
They are much more likely to drop when the player is in more biomes, replacing the chance for normal jewels to drop

LUCKY (Forest/Underground): Major/Minor, alongside normal stants, grant a small amount of luck, and are easier to upgrade, more likely to be cut successfully, and cannot be broken by pliers

OPALS (Jungle/Glowing Mushroom): Major/Minor, but contain only sub stats. Each upgrade improves two random sub stats

PEARL (Ocean): Major/Minor, but contain only trigger effects (3 for major, 1 for minor)

SOULSTONE (Dungeon): Major/Minor, but do not use stats. Instead, they spawn a ghostly wisp, which can be collected. Collecting them enough will shoot them towards a nearby enemy, causing a massively impactful debuff:
1. Soulstone of Agony - Double damage taken
2. Soulstone of Grief - Heal damaging players
3. Soulstone of Sacrifice - Deal a massive damage over time based on max health
4. Soulstone of Betrayal - Friendly units blow up and hurt the target when killed
5. Soulstone of Plague - Halved behavior speed
6. Soulstone of Torture - Massive damage dealt reduction

SPECTROLITE (Snow): Major/Minor, but instead of using stats, they summon a helpful active minion. The minion does not use summon slots, and using two or more Spectrolite will spawn multiple minions.
Fairy: Heals the player for a small amount
Beetle: Reduces the next damage taken by 15%, stacks with other beetles but not itself
Cactus: A portion of the damage taken is redirected to the attacker, with the redirected damage portion being healed back to the player, stacks uses not effect strength
Heart slime: Grants a temporary heart that absorbs a certain amount of damage before disappearing, stacks with other heart slimes but not itself
Kitten: Grants increased regeneration until the next hit, stacks with other kittens but not itself
Creeper: Takes damage for the player, blocking projectiles, and dies itself after taking enough hits
